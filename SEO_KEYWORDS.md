## Phrases to avoid on-page

- “Guaranteed #1 on Google”
- Fake star ratings / fabricated review quotes in schema
- Exact-match spam paragraphs repeating “best jewellery shop in Sikar” endlessly
- Invented PIN codes, awards, or branch addresses
