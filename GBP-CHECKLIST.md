## Do not

- Put “Best jeweller in Sikar” inside the **business name**
- Create fake reviews, or offer any incentive (discount, gift, cash, lucky draw) for a review
- Ask only happy customers, or route people through a "were you satisfied?" step first (review gating)
- Draft or AI-write review text for customers
- Use a different phone/PIN than the website
- Mark the business as open 24/7 or as an online store
